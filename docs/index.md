# OtakuWorld

New here? This page explains which file to download, what each app does, and how to get
something to read or watch. You don't need to know anything about programming.

## Which app do I want?

OtakuWorld is a family of three separate apps. Install only the ones you want.

| App            | What it's for                                       | Android | Desktop |
|----------------|-----------------------------------------------------|---------|---------|
| **MangaWorld** | Reading manga, manhwa, and comics                   | ✅       | ✅       |
| **AnimeWorld** | Watching and downloading anime, shows, and movies   | ✅       | ✅       |
| **NovelWorld** | Reading web novels and light novels                 | ✅       | ✅       |

All three share the same layout: **Recent** (new releases), **All** (browse), **Favorites**,
**Notifications**, and **Settings**. Learn one and you know them all.

> **Important:** the apps come empty. They don't include any manga, anime, or novels. You add
> *extensions* (also called *sources*) that tell the app where to find content. See
> [Extensions](extensions.md).

### MangaWorld

- Read chapters in a built-in reader: scrolling list, page by page, or page-flip
- Download chapters to read offline
- Favorite a title to get a notification when a new chapter comes out
- Works with Mihon / Tachiyomi extensions

### AnimeWorld

- Stream episodes in a built-in player, with subtitles you can restyle
- Download episodes to watch offline (subtitles are kept)
- Cast to a Chromecast or Google TV (Android only)
- Works with Aniyomi extensions and CloudStream plugins

### NovelWorld

- Read chapters in a built-in reader with adjustable text size
- Optional "Previously, in…" recap at the top of each chapter (needs on-device AI)
