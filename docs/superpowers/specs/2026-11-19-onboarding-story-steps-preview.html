Step 1

Welcome

On screen

  • "Welcome to {app}" + one line saying it takes about a minute and everything can change later
  • Five genre cards dealt into a fan
  • "Coming up" chips: Look, Layout, Sources, Reader/Player, Sync, Updates
  • Buttons: Back (disabled), "Let's go". Skip at top right.

Motion

MomentSpecCompose

ChipsStagger in after title, 45ms apartpagerStagger

Decided

Genre cards: icon + genre name on gradient cards, five per app. MangaWorld: Action, Romance, Mystery, Comedy, Fantasy. AnimeWorld: Action, Romance, Sci-fi, Slice of life, Fantasy. NovelWorld: Fantasy, Romance, Mystery, Isekai, Adventure. No art assets needed.

Step 4

Sources

On screen

  • One line on what an extension is: it connects the app to a site with titles
  • How it works, 3 steps that highlight in turn: Find (Settings › Sources & Extensions › Get extensions), Install, Browse
  • Where extensions come from, per app. All apps: OtakuWorld catalog (built in). MangaWorld: Mihon & Tachiyomi repos. AnimeWorld: Aniyomi repos, CloudStream plugins. Custom repos only when the app has a custom bridge (hasCustomBridge).
  • Explain only: no buttons, no language picker. A closing line says extensions are added after setup from Settings › Sources & Extensions. Language order stays in the Extensions screen.
